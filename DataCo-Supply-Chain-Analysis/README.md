# DataCo Supply Chain Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze supply chain operations, sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior and delivery performance using the DataCo Supply Chain dataset.

The dashboard transforms raw data into interactive visual reports to help explore business performance, identify delivery issues and understand trends across products, customers, markets and geographic regions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, order volume and customer performance.
- Evaluate product and customer segment profitability.
- Monitor late deliveries and on-time delivery performance.
- Compare actual shipping time with scheduled shipping time.
- Explore sales and order distribution across geographic regions.
- Track year-over-year performance using DAX measures.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Key performance indicators for sales, profit, orders, customers and profit margin.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Sales and profit comparisons across markets and product categories.
- Delivery performance overview.
- Year-over-year performance indicators.

### 2. Logistics & Delivery Analysis
- Late orders and late delivery percentage.
- Delivery performance by shipping mode and region.
- Actual versus scheduled shipping time.
- Shipping mode performance summary.
- Interactive filters for year, market, region and shipping mode.

### 3. Product & Customer Analysis
- Sales by customer segment.
- Profitability by product category.
- Top 10 products by profit.
- Sales by department.
- Product-level sales, profit, profit margin and units sold.

### 4. Geographic & Regional Analysis
- Geographic visualization using latitude and longitude.
- Order distribution across locations.
- Regional and country-level sales analysis.
- Interactive exploration of geographic sales and delivery performance.

## How to Explore the Project

1. Download the Power BI `.pbix` and `.csv` file from this repository.
2. Open it using Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers to filter results by year, market, customer segment, region, category or shipping mode, where available.
4. Explore each report page to analyze business performance from different perspectives.

## Dataset

The project uses the DataCo Supply Chain dataset, which contains order, product, customer, sales, profitability, shipping, delivery status and geographic information.
